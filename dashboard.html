Puzzle League

Your friends

Pick up to 10 people from your leaderboards. They’ll show on every game’s track and in the league table.

Saving results

When you open a game’s leaderboard after playing, yesterday’s final standings are saved automatically.

If a day was missed, Puzzle League briefly opens that game’s leaderboard in a background tab and closes it again. It only does this after you’ve played today’s puzzle, or on Saturday’s puzzle day, so it never starts a puzzle timer for you.

Your data

Everything is stored in this browser only.
